# How to Use the Paper Collage Master Prompt

## Step 1 — Copy the master prompt

Open `paper-collage-master-prompt.md`, select everything, and copy it.

## Step 2 — Paste it into an AI chat

Paste it into **ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini**. The prompt will introduce itself and ask you 6 questions:

1. What is the topic?
2. Which AI video tool will you use? (Veo / Sora / Runway / Pika / Kling / Luma / Other)
3. How many prompts (clips) do you need?
4. Who is the audience? (kids, students, beginners, professionals)
5. Voiceover, on-screen text only, or both? In which language?
6. Any key points, facts, or colors that must be included?

**Tip:** If you're unsure about any answer, just say "you decide" — the prompt suggests defaults and continues.

## Step 3 — Approve the story outline

After you answer, it shows you a story outline (Hook → Explanation → Example → Takeaway) divided across your clips. Read it, ask for changes if you want, then approve.

## Step 4 — Get your per-clip prompts

You'll get one complete, copy-paste prompt per clip. Each includes:

- The full prompt text (style, subject, camera)
- A second-by-second visual breakdown
- Sound design — each effect, its timing, and what triggers it
- The voiceover line (if you chose voiceover)
- The ending frame description (so the next clip connects smoothly)

## Step 5 — Generate each clip

Paste each prompt into your AI video tool:

| Tool | Clip length |
|---|---|
| Veo | exactly 10 seconds |
| Sora | 5–10 seconds |
| Runway | 5–10 seconds |
| Pika / Kling / Luma | 5 seconds |

Voiceover tip: the voiceover line is sized for the clip (about 2 words per second — roughly 20 words for a 10-second clip).

## Step 6 — Assemble

Follow the **assembly guide** at the end: the clip order, transitions, and optional voiceover. Match each clip's ending frame to the next clip's opening for a smooth story.

## Pro tips

- **One idea per clip.** Don't cram — that's the #1 quality killer.
- **Captions max 6 words.** Short text reads better on screen.
- **Keep the palette consistent.** The prompt locks the palette across all clips so nothing looks off.
- **Sound matters.** Paper rustle on every slide, a tear on every title reveal — it sells the handmade feel.
